We work directly with licensed manufacturers to bring you fresh stock, fair prices and a huge variety — from gentle sparklers for kids to grand aerial shows for weddings.

Every product we sell is safety-tested and compliant with explosive regulations, so you can celebrate with complete peace of mind.

A Few Fireworks Safety Tips

👀

Adult Supervision

Always keep children under adult supervision while bursting crackers.

💧

Keep Water Nearby

Keep a bucket of water or sand close by for emergencies.

🌬️

Open Spaces Only

Burst fireworks only in open, well-ventilated outdoor areas.
